- **Reconnaissance:** Strengthened my ability to map an application's attack surface beyond what is visible in the normal UI.
- **Vulnerability Verification:** Learned the importance of confirming the actual security impact of a suspected vulnerability rather than stopping after identifying an unusual response.
- **Continuous Learning:** Reinforced the importance of testing authentication and access control mechanisms thoroughly across different applications.
